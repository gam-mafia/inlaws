# Family portrait hex sticker concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are raster design concepts; edge and transparency cleanup is needed before printing, particularly for the green variant.

## portrait-friendly

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished hex sticker design for R package inlaws, which adds distribution families to mgcv. Family portrait theme: three anthropomorphic statistical distribution silhouettes posed close together as relatives, tall right-skewed density, broad rounded bell density, and small discrete count-bar child. Shapes must visibly read as statistical distributions, with tiny simple faces. Flat vector-like illustration, bold clean outlines, minimal detail readable on a 5 cm sticker. Regular point-up hexagon, complete uncut border, centered front-on, no perspective or mockup. Exact text only "inlaws" in large lowercase lettering across lower portion. Transparent outside hexagon, opaque interior. No other text, watermark, shadows outside sticker, or extra panels. Friendly contemporary portrait. Warm cream interior, dark teal thick border and typography, coral and muted golden yellow characters with dark teal outlines. Three charming relatives stand shoulder to shoulder, tallest behind, smallest count bars in front. Rounded confident sans serif wordmark. Restrained, clever, cozy.
